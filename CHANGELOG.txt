10/14/2026 #3 (1.2.0):
This is a big pathfinding update for Lethal Access! Pathfinding now moves you like a real player would, gets past obstacles, jumps gaps, crawls under server racks, and helps you do all of that yourself in manual mode. Read below:

* Automatic pathfinding now walks and runs you the way the game does, with normal animations, footsteps, stamina and gravity. No more floating or double speed when you also hold a movement key. Turn running off with Auto Pathfinding Sprint in the F10 menu. It steers smoothly across open ground and keeps the camera level.
* Automatic pathfinding gets past obstacles on its own: it jumps low walls, railings and short steep rocks, crouches under low beams, steps around crates and corners, opens doors, and climbs ladders up and down.
* Gaps, like the girder rooms in the factory, are now jumped for real instead of skipped. Automatic pathfinding works out a jump that will land, takes a run-up and jumps at the edge. If the run-up goes wrong, it stops and tries again.
* Manual pathfinding helps you jump gaps yourself. It tells you how wide the gap is and whether to walk or sprint, guides you onto the run-up line, and ticks faster toward the edge with a high tone when it's time to jump. A jump that's too early is held until the right moment, one that wouldn't make it is held back with the reason, and holding forward in the air steers you to the landing.
* Manual pathfinding also calls out what's coming up in a word or two: "Jump", "Crouch", "Stand up", which side a ladder is on, and which way to step when you're walking into something. It stops you walking off a high drop and says "Ledge".
* Pathfinding goes round places a player can't get through, like slopes too steep to walk up and gaps too low to crawl under. If you get stuck, it remembers the spot and finds another way. If a target can't be reached, it says "Cannot get any closer".
* Pathfinding takes the Mineshaft elevator. Automatic mode calls it, steps in, presses the button and steps out. Manual mode tells you each step and turns you to face each button.
* Automatic mode gets through low, rocky tunnels: it angles across rock too steep to walk straight up, stands up to jump a lip where there's room, and backs out of tight spots to try again. Manual mode says "Stand up and jump" at a lip, and angles your steps side to side when steep rock stops you dead.
* Pathfinding crawls under server racks. Automatic mode lines up, crouches, crawls through and stands up. Manual mode says "Crouch, crawl space", keeps you lined up, and tells you when to stand up.
* With a key in your inventory, pathfinding routes through a locked door, stops at it and tells you to use your key.
* Pathfinding avoids quicksand, warns you about deep water, finds the way up to raised places like the main entrance on Experimentation, recalculates if you wander off, and no longer teleports you.
* The game now says "Crouched", "Standing", "Sprinting" and "Walking" when that changes, including during automatic pathfinding. Looking at a door reads its "Use door" prompt. Arriving says "Reached" once instead of twice.
* Fall damage is only turned off while automatic pathfinding is walking you, not for the rest of the session.
* The game now goes straight to the main menu, skipping the boot-up screen, cold open scenes and menu animation. Turn these off with Skip Intro Screens and Disable Cold Open Events in the F10 menu.
* When you host, the ship lands and takes off three times faster. Change it with Ship Landing Speed, or turn it off with Fast Ship Landing.
* Picking up a flashlight, belt bag or other one-handed tool is announced again (it goes to the utility slot, which the announcement skipped with an error).

10/11/2026 #2 (1.1.1):
- Fixed the game closing about 20 seconds after starting on a fresh install. BepInEx's default settings let Lethal Company unload every mod at the launch screen, and LethalAccess then crashed the game while speaking. LethalAccess now keeps itself loaded whatever that setting says.
- The updater now fixes that BepInEx setting (HideManagerGameObject in BepInEx.cfg) whenever it runs, so other mods stay loaded too.

10/11/2026 #1 (1.1.0):
- New LethalAccess Updater. Download LethalAccessUpdater.exe from the releases page and run it. It finds Lethal Company through Steam, installs BepInEx if it's missing, asks if you want InputUtils, then installs LethalAccess. Run it again any time to update. It also repairs your install if any LethalAccess files are missing or changed, and waits for the game to close before changing anything.
- The updater can follow Beta builds. Run it with --beta to switch to Beta, and --stable to switch back.
- Menus now go through your screen reader directly. LethalAccess shows the game's menus to Windows as real controls, so your screen reader reads them in its own words, the same way it reads any other program.
- Text fields can now be reviewed with your screen reader. The arrow keys, Home and End read where you moved to, and password fields read their mask instead of what you typed.
- Each menu screen now says its name when you arrive. Prompts like the unsaved changes prompt announce as dialogs, and the news pop-up keeps focus until you close it.
- Tab now moves one control at a time, and Ctrl+Tab moves by section. Tab also leaves a text field, so Enter is no longer the only way out.
- Escape now backs out of menu screens, the same as pressing Back.
- Save file delete buttons are now right after the save they delete, and announce as destructive.
- The keybinds screen is now fully accessible. It opens on the first keybind instead of the Back button, and every row, rebind prompt, reset and dropdown is announced. Reset all no longer moves you to the Back button, and Escape closes an open dropdown instead of leaving the screen.
- With InputUtils installed, the LethalAccess keys now have their own LethalAccess section on the game's keybinds screen, where you can rebind, reset and save them like any other key. Without InputUtils, you can still change them in the F10 menu.
- Removed the elevator ray keybind, which was only for development.
