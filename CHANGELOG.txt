10/11/2026 #3 (1.2.0):
This is a big pathfinding update for Lethal Access! Pathfinding now moves you like a real player would, gets past obstacles, jumps gaps, crawls under server racks, and helps you do all of that yourself in manual mode. Read below:

* Automatic pathfinding now walks and runs you the way the game does, with normal animations, footsteps, stamina and gravity. No more floating, and no more double speed when you also hold a movement key. Turn running off with Auto Pathfinding Sprint in the F10 menu.
* Automatic pathfinding gets past obstacles on its own. It jumps over low walls and railings and up short steep rocks, crouches under low beams, steps around crates and corners, opens doors, and climbs ladders up and down.
* Automatic pathfinding steers smoothly, cutting across open ground instead of turning at every point, and keeps the camera level while it walks.
* Gaps, like the girder rooms in the factory, are now jumped for real instead of skipped over. Automatic pathfinding works out a jump that will land, takes a run-up, and jumps at the edge. If the run-up goes wrong, it stops and tries again instead of jumping short.
* Manual pathfinding helps you jump gaps yourself. It tells you how wide the gap is and whether to walk or sprint, guides you to your run-up and onto the right line, and ticks faster as you near the edge, with a high tone when it's time to jump. A slightly early jump is held until the right moment, a jump that wouldn't make it is held back with the reason why, and holding forward in the air steers you to the landing.
* Manual pathfinding tells you what's coming up, in a word or two: "Jump", "Crouch", "Stand up" once there's room again, which side a ladder is on, and which way to step when you're walking into something. It also stops you walking off the edge of a high drop and says "Ledge".
* Stairs no longer set off false "Jump", "Crouch", "Ledge" or gap warnings, and pathfinding no longer says you've arrived from the floor above the target.
* Getting on and off the ship works in both modes, using the ship's ladders. Pathfinding no longer tries to climb the side of the ship.
* Pathfinding goes round places a player can't get through, like slopes too steep to walk up and gaps too low to crawl under, and if you get stuck somewhere it remembers the spot and finds another way.
* Pathfinding takes the elevator in the Mineshaft. Automatic mode calls it, steps in, presses the button and steps out at the other end. Manual mode tells you each step and turns you to face each button, so pressing interact uses it.
* When a gap is too far to jump, pathfinding says "Can't make that jump. Finding another way." and goes round instead of giving up.
* Automatic pathfinding sprints at a gap when a walking jump would only just make it, and stops trying a jump that keeps landing back where it started.
* Automatic pathfinding hops railings straight on instead of sideways, and won't walk off a big drop the path doesn't take, like the pit beside a narrow beam.
* If a ladder won't take you, automatic pathfinding says "Can't get on this ladder. Finding another way." instead of waiting at it.
* Pathfinding walks round the edge of low things, like the ship's hull, when there's room to stand a few steps away, instead of crawling under them.
* Pathfinding goes round rock faces too steep to walk up, even where the slope is uneven, instead of sending you up them.
* Manual mode no longer tells you to jump onto a narrow beam over a big drop. It finds another way, or tells you there's no safe way on.
* Manual mode only lets you run off the edge of a gap when you're moving at it and right at the jump, so stopping at the edge, or lining up for the next gap, no longer drops you in. On a sprint jump it eases off your sprint during the run-up, so you reach the edge at the speed the jump needs. When there's no safe jump, it finds another way.
* When you're stuck at a rock lip in manual mode, it says "Jump" instead of telling you to step aside. "Ledge" and "Blocked" no longer repeat endlessly in the same place.
* Automatic mode gets through low, rocky tunnels: it angles across rock too steep to walk straight up, stands up to jump a lip where there's room, and angles off or backs out of a tight spot to try again. Manual mode says "Stand up and jump" when you're crouched at a lip. When you're stopped dead walking into steep rock or a low rocky tunnel in manual mode, your steps are angled a little to one side and then the other until you get past.
* Pathfinding crawls under server racks. Automatic mode lines up, crouches, crawls through and stands up on the other side. Manual mode says "Crouch, crawl space", keeps you lined up, and tells you when to stand up.
* Automatic pathfinding keeps some stamina in reserve instead of sprinting until you're exhausted, so it can still jump when it reaches a ledge or gap. Before a running jump it waits a moment to get its breath back if it needs to.
* The game now says "Crouched", "Standing", "Sprinting" and "Walking" whenever that changes, including while automatic pathfinding moves you.
* Looking at a door now reads its "Use door" prompt.
* Arriving somewhere you were pathfinding to says "Reached" once, instead of twice with two different names.
* With a key in your inventory, pathfinding can route through a locked door. It stops at the door and tells you to use your key, then carries on.
* Pathfinding now avoids quicksand, warns you about deep water, finds the way up to raised places like the main entrance on Experimentation, recalculates if you wander off, and no longer teleports you anywhere. If a target can't be reached, it says "Cannot get any closer".
* Fall damage is now only turned off while automatic pathfinding is walking you, instead of for the rest of the session.
* The game now goes straight to the main menu, skipping the boot-up screen, cold open scenes and menu animation. Turn these off with Skip Intro Screens and Disable Cold Open Events in the F10 menu.
* When you host, the ship lands and takes off three times faster. Change this with Ship Landing Speed, or turn it off with Fast Ship Landing.
* Picking up a flashlight, belt bag or any other one-handed tool is announced again. These go to the utility slot, which the announcement skipped with an error.

10/11/2026 #2 (1.1.1):
- Fixed the game closing about 20 seconds after starting on a fresh install. BepInEx's default settings let Lethal Company unload every mod at the launch screen, and LethalAccess then crashed the game while speaking. LethalAccess now keeps itself loaded whatever that setting says.
- The updater now fixes that BepInEx setting (HideManagerGameObject in BepInEx.cfg) whenever it runs, so other mods stay loaded too.

10/11/2026 #1 (1.1.0):
- New LethalAccess Updater. Download LethalAccessUpdater.exe from the releases page and run it. It finds Lethal Company through Steam, installs BepInEx if it's missing, asks if you want InputUtils, then installs LethalAccess. Run it again any time to update. It also repairs your install if any LethalAccess files are missing or changed, and waits for the game to close before changing anything.
- The updater can follow Beta builds. Run it with --beta to switch to Beta, and --stable to switch back.
- Menus now go through your screen reader directly. LethalAccess shows the game's menus to Windows as real controls, so your screen reader reads them in its own words, the same way it reads any other program.
- Text fields can now be reviewed with your screen reader. The arrow keys, Home and End read where you moved to, and password fields read their mask instead of what you typed.
- Each menu screen now says its name when you arrive. Prompts like the unsaved changes prompt announce as dialogs, and the news pop-up keeps focus until you close it.
- Tab now moves one control at a time, and Ctrl+Tab moves by section. Tab also leaves a text field, so Enter is no longer the only way out.
- Escape now backs out of menu screens, the same as pressing Back.
- Save file delete buttons are now right after the save they delete, and announce as destructive.
- The keybinds screen is now fully accessible. It opens on the first keybind instead of the Back button, and every row, rebind prompt, reset and dropdown is announced. Reset all no longer moves you to the Back button, and Escape closes an open dropdown instead of leaving the screen.
- With InputUtils installed, the LethalAccess keys now have their own LethalAccess section on the game's keybinds screen, where you can rebind, reset and save them like any other key. Without InputUtils, you can still change them in the F10 menu.
- Removed the elevator ray keybind, which was only for development.
