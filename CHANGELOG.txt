10/11/2026 #3 (1.2.0):
- Path points on moons now sit on the ground. They used to sink into hills between turns of the path.
- Pathfinding starts right away, with no wait and no "generating path" messages.
- Automatic pathfinding now walks you like a player does: normal walking and running animations, footsteps and gravity, and no more floating. It no longer doubles your speed when you also hold a movement key.
- Automatic pathfinding runs. Running uses stamina, and you slow to a walk when you're exhausted. Turn this off with Auto Pathfinding Sprint in the F10 menu.
- Automatic pathfinding now gets past obstacles the way a player does. It jumps over low walls and railings, crouches under low beams, steps around crates and corners, and climbs ladders. It faces along the path and strafes, so getting around something doesn't need a full turn.
- Manual pathfinding tells you what to do: "Jump" before something you can jump over, "Crouch" before a low beam, "Stand up" once you're past it, which side a ladder is on, and which way to step when you're walking into something. Walking into the ladder on your path grabs it, so you don't have to aim at it.
- Pathfinding opens doors the way you would: only the door it's walking through, after holding interact on it for the door's hold time, so it no longer opens doors beside or behind you. It also follows the game's rules for ladders, so it won't climb one while you carry a two-handed item and tells you to drop it.
- Automatic pathfinding steers smoothly: it heads for the furthest point along the path it can walk to in a straight line, skipping nodes and cutting across open ground instead of turning at every node.
- Automatic pathfinding keeps the camera level while walking and turns it to face the target when it arrives.
- Pathfinding climbs down ladders when the drop is more than a few metres, in both modes, and lets go at the bottom when the ladder ends at the floor.
- With a key in your inventory, pathfinding can route through a locked door when that's much shorter or the only way. It stops at the door, points the camera at it, and tells you to use your key, then carries on once it's unlocked.
- Both modes plan around quicksand and warn you about quicksand or deep water ahead. Automatic pathfinding keeps moving in water instead of stopping to wait for stamina.
- Pathfinding avoids the climbs that only monsters can make, and paths keep a little distance from walls and railings.
- Pathfinding to something on a raised platform, like the main entrance on Experimentation, now finds the way up instead of stopping on the ground below. If a target really can't be reached, it says "Cannot get any closer" instead of "Reached".
- Pathfinding recalculates the path if you wander far from it, and automatic pathfinding gets itself unstuck.
- Fall damage is now only switched off while automatic pathfinding walks you. Before, it stayed off for the rest of the session after your first pathfind.
- The game now goes straight to the main menu after you pick Online or LAN, with no boot-up screen, no cold open scenes and no menu opening animation. Turn these off with Skip Intro Screens and Disable Cold Open Events.
- When you host, the ship lands and takes off three times faster. Change the speed with Ship Landing Speed, or turn it off with Fast Ship Landing.

10/11/2026 #2 (1.1.1):
- Fixed the game closing about 20 seconds after starting on a fresh install. BepInEx's default settings let Lethal Company unload every mod at the launch screen, and LethalAccess then crashed the game while speaking. LethalAccess now keeps itself loaded whatever that setting says.
- The updater now fixes that BepInEx setting (HideManagerGameObject in BepInEx.cfg) whenever it runs, so other mods stay loaded too.

10/11/2026 #1 (1.1.0):
- New LethalAccess Updater. Download LethalAccessUpdater.exe from the releases page and run it. It finds Lethal Company through Steam, installs BepInEx if it's missing, asks if you want InputUtils, then installs LethalAccess. Run it again any time to update. It also repairs your install if any LethalAccess files are missing or changed, and waits for the game to close before changing anything.
- The updater can follow Beta builds. Run it with --beta to switch to Beta, and --stable to switch back.
- Menus now go through your screen reader directly. LethalAccess shows the game's menus to Windows as real controls, so your screen reader reads them in its own words, the same way it reads any other program.
- Text fields can now be reviewed with your screen reader. The arrow keys, Home and End read where you moved to, and password fields read their mask instead of what you typed.
- Each menu screen now says its name when you arrive. Prompts like the unsaved changes prompt announce as dialogs, and the news pop-up keeps focus until you close it.
- Tab now moves one control at a time, and Ctrl+Tab moves by section. Tab also leaves a text field, so Enter is no longer the only way out.
- Escape now backs out of menu screens, the same as pressing Back.
- Save file delete buttons are now right after the save they delete, and announce as destructive.
- The keybinds screen is now fully accessible. It opens on the first keybind instead of the Back button, and every row, rebind prompt, reset and dropdown is announced. Reset all no longer moves you to the Back button, and Escape closes an open dropdown instead of leaving the screen.
- With InputUtils installed, the LethalAccess keys now have their own LethalAccess section on the game's keybinds screen, where you can rebind, reset and save them like any other key. Without InputUtils, you can still change them in the F10 menu.
- Removed the elevator ray keybind, which was only for development.
